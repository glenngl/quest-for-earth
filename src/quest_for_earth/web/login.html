Quest for Earth

Level 1 — a browser space scavenger. Sign in or create a pilot.
Change password (if you know the old one)

Controls

WS
thrust forward / back (aim with mouse)
Mouse
aim your view (click the canvas to lock)
Click
fire at the locked target
↑↓←→
aim without the mouse
E
evade — burst away from a threat
R
repair a hurt wingman
U
use an energy cell (repair your shields)
F
harvest a rock in reach
T
affiliate with a squadmate
Y
share your scan chart with your wingman
V
toggle camera (third person / cockpit)
M
trim the HUD
H
toggle the in-game help overlay
F2
fullscreen
Fly over salvage to grab it. Fly into the wormhole to enter it. Asteroids block your view — a ship on the far side of a rock is hidden.
